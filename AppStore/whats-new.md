# What's New

## 2.0

DayByDay now turns every topic into games.

- Games in every topic: Find it!, odd one out, and listen-and-pick, with cheers for right answers and a gentle hint for wrong ones.
- Order and counting: put the week train in order, spin the year wheel, sort yesterday, today and tomorrow, count objects and find the bigger pile.
- Richer cards: animal sounds and facts, letter sounds with an example word, tracing letters and shapes, mixing colors, dressing for the weather, shaking the device to make it rain, sorting fruit and vegetables, and movement prompts like "Touch your nose!".
- A daily Today routine: hear the day, month and season, pick today's weather, and add a sticker to the calendar.
- A friendly owl who asks the questions and cheers your child on.
- A profile for each child, with their own stickers, birthday and day pictures such as swim day or grandma day.
- Practice adapts: anything a child misses comes up more often until they know it.
- A Grown-Ups area behind a parent gate shows what each child knows and what they are still mixing up.
- A new recorded voice for every word and prompt, so it sounds the same on every device.

Everything stays on the device: no accounts, ads, tracking or internet connection.
