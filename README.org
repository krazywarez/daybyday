#+title: day by day

support this project through [[https://github.com/sponsors/ccleberg][github sponsors]].

* what
ios app of learning games for children aged 4 and 5. eleven topics:
days, months, seasons, numbers, colors, shapes, alphabet, animals,
weather, body parts, and food.

* does
- big cards for every topic, each word spoken in a recorded voice
- games in every topic: find it, odd one out, listen and pick
- ordering and counting: week train, year wheel, yesterday/today/tomorrow,
  counting objects, bigger pile
- tracing letters and shapes, mixing colors, dressing for the weather
- a daily today routine: day, month, season, weather, and a calendar sticker
- an owl who asks the questions and cheers
- a profile for each child; practice repeats what a child misses
- a grown-ups area behind a parent gate shows what each child knows
- voiceover, voice control, and larger text
- large tap targets. no reading required
- offline. no network, no accounts, no ads, no tracking

* requires
ios 26 or later.

* release
=scripts/take-screenshots.sh= retakes =AppStore/screenshots= on the iPhone 18 Pro Max
and iPad Pro 13-inch (M5) simulators; the 6.3" set is resized from the 6.9" one.
=TEXT_SIZE=accessibility-extra-extra-extra-large scripts/take-screenshots.sh= takes
the same set at the largest text size, into
=AppStore/screenshots-accessibility-extra-extra-extra-large=.

The =DayByDayUITests= target opens every screen by its VoiceOver labels and runs
Xcode's accessibility audit on it, at the default and the largest text size.

=scripts/release-ios.sh= archives the app and uploads it to App Store Connect for
TestFlight, from a clean =main=, signed through the Apple ID in Xcode (Xcode >
Settings > Accounts). The version is =MARKETING_VERSION=; the build number is the
commit count, so each upload needs a new commit. =DAYBYDAY_PUBLISH=0= exports the
=.ipa= to =.build/ios-release= without uploading. Release notes are in
=AppStore/whats-new.md=.

=scripts/render-header.sh= renders the product page headers (21:9 and 16:9)
from =AppStore/header.html= and the iPhone screenshots, with Chromium and ImageMagick.
