#+title: day by day

* what
ios app for young children. days of the week, months, and seasons
as large cards. tap a card to hear it read aloud.

* does
- days, months, and seasons as big colorful cards
- tap to hear it, apple on-device text-to-speech
- today's day, current month, and current season are highlighted
- built for ages 4-5. large tap targets. no reading required
- offline. no network, no accounts, no ads

* requires
ios 26 or later. for better audio, download an enhanced voice under
Settings > Accessibility > Read & Speak > Voices.

* release
=scripts/take-screenshots.sh= retakes =AppStore/screenshots= on the iPhone 18 Pro Max
and iPad Pro 13-inch (M5) simulators; the 6.3" set is resized from the 6.9" one.

=scripts/release-ios.sh= archives the app and uploads it to App Store Connect for
TestFlight, from a clean =main=, signed through the Apple ID in Xcode (Xcode >
Settings > Accounts). The version is =MARKETING_VERSION=; the build number is the
commit count, so each upload needs a new commit. =DAYBYDAY_PUBLISH=0= exports the
=.ipa= to =.build/ios-release= without uploading. Release notes are in
=AppStore/whats-new.md=.

=scripts/render-header.sh= renders the product page headers (21:9 and 16:9)
from =AppStore/header.html= and the iPhone screenshots, with Chromium and ImageMagick.
